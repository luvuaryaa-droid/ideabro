# Brief: acrion-promo

> Ideabro Studio · Vibe Editing System.

## 1. The job
- **Video type:** launch / brand promo
- **Client:** Acrion Realty Pvt Ltd (acrionrealty.com), residential real estate, Bhubaneswar
- **One-line goal:** make home-seekers in Bhubaneswar remember Acrion Realty and visit acrionrealty.com
- **Platform(s):** Instagram Reels / YouTube Shorts / TikTok
- **Format:** 9:16 (1080×1920) @ 30 fps. Other versions: on request (4:5, 1:1, 16:9)
- **Length:** 22.5 s (12 bars at 128 BPM)

## 2. Message
- **Hook:** "STILL SEARCHING FOR THE RIGHT HOME?"

- **Must-avoid:** prices, sizes, RERA numbers or any claim the client hasn't supplied

## 3. Vibe
- **Vibe words:** catchy, fast, premium
- **Pace:** fast (hard cuts on bars, word slams on beats)
- **Music:** original `hype` bed (128 BPM, F minor) from audio_kit, music only, no voice
